Every agent, tool and MCP hop in n8n, Zapier, LangGraph and CrewAI, mapped and owned.

How it works

Live in four steps.

  1. 01Connect

    Register workflows, agents, MCP servers and A2A peers through the SDK, proxy or gateway.

  2. 02Monitor

    Zenstra learns each workflow's normal path, tools and data reach.

  3. 03Detect

    Injection, escalation, poisoning and identity anomalies are scored in real time.

  4. 04Enforce

    Allow, step up to a person, or block and quarantine before the hop lands.

FAQ

Questions teams ask first.

ISO 27001 · SOC 2 Type II · ISO/IEC 42001 · ISO/IEC 27701 · Compliance

Do we have to rebuild our workflows?

No. Zenstra connects through the SDK, a proxy or the gateway, with no agent rewrite.

What happens when a hop is blocked?

The next step does not run. The decision, the policy that made it and the full run are recorded.

Does it slow workflows down?

Decisions are made inline in 18 ms at p95.
