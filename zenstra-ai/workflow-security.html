FAQ

Questions workflow owners ask first.

ISO 27001 · SOC 2 Type II · ISO/IEC 42001 · ISO/IEC 27701 · Compliance

Do we have to rebuild our workflows?

No. Workflows connect through the proxy, the SDK or the gateway. The agents, the workflow, the model and the cloud stay as they are.

What is the “declared task”?

The goal a run was started for, such as answering one member about one fee. Every hop is checked against it, so a step that is allowed in general can still be stopped when it does not serve this run.

What happens when a hop is blocked or held?

A blocked hop does not run, and the rest of the workflow carries on where it can. A held hop waits for an approver with the whole run in front of them. Both are written to the decision record.

Does it slow workflows down?

Decisions are made inline, at 18 ms at the 95th percentile.

What if Zenstra cannot be reached?

It fails closed: the hop is held, never waved through.

How do we find workflows nobody told us about?

Unregistered agents and no-code workflows are surfaced with their owner and the signal that found them, so they can be brought under policy.
