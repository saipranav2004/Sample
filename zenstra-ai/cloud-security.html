FAQ

Questions architects ask first.

ISO 27001 · SOC 2 Type II · ISO/IEC 42001 · ISO/IEC 27701 · Compliance

Does any prompt or payload leave our cloud?

No. Prompts, completions, payloads and credentials stay in your environment. Only decisions and redacted evidence reach the Zenstra control plane.

Which clouds and model services does it support?

AWS, Azure and Google Cloud, on EKS, AKS or GKE, in front of Amazon Bedrock, Azure OpenAI, Vertex AI, your MCP servers and internal APIs.

How is it deployed?

With the Helm chart or the Terraform module, into your own account, alongside the rest of your infrastructure code.

Do our agents need code changes?

No rewrite. Agents call the gateway in place of the service, and each one is registered with its own identity and grants.

What happens if the gateway cannot reach Zenstra?

It fails closed: calls are held, never waved through.

How much latency does it add?

Decisions are made inline, at 18 ms at the 95th percentile.
