FAQ

Questions SOC and endpoint teams ask first.

ISO 27001 · SOC 2 Type II · ISO/IEC 42001 · ISO/IEC 27701 · Compliance

Does it replace our EDR?

No. Your EDR keeps watching processes, files and the network. Zenstra EDR adds what it cannot see: the prompt and the task behind an AI tool’s actions.

Which AI tools and operating systems are covered?

Microsoft Copilot, ChatGPT, Claude Code, Cursor and unregistered agents, on Windows and macOS.

How is it rolled out?

Through the device management you already run: Microsoft Intune, Jamf or Kandji.

Will it get in developers’ way?

It acts on the agent, not the person. A blocked action or an ended agent session leaves the developer, the editor and the machine working.

What about AI tools we have not approved?

Unsanctioned AI tools and agents are detected, and your team alerted, the moment one appears on a device.
