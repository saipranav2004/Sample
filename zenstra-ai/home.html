How a decision happens

One action, followed
from start to finish.

Follow a loan officer’s AI assistant through five checks, from the moment Zenstra meets it to the note the officer sees.

01 · Identity

Every agent gets a name and an owner.

Zenstra finds the AI agents working on devices and in workflows, including ones nobody registered, and ties each one to the person and team responsible.

02 · Intent

Is this what it was asked to do?

Catch an underwriting or fraud agent the moment it reaches beyond its declared task, before a bad decision lands in a member’s file.

03 · Policy

Your rules, attached where the action happens.

A policy written once is checked on every action. Changing a risk score needs a person, so this one waits for a reviewer instead of reaching the file.

04 · Protect

Sensitive data stops at the device.

The officer pastes a system login into an AI tool. Zenstra EDR recognises the credentials and blocks the send on the laptop, before it leaves.

05 · Record

A simple answer up front. The full story behind it.

The officer sees one short note per action. Your security team sees the whole record: who acted, what they tried, which rule decided and why.
