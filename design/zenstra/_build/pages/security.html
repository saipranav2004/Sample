Data handling

What stays with you, and what never leaves.

The decision point runs in your environment. This is the full list of what reaches Zenstra's cloud, so your privacy office can check it against the data flow diagram.

Stays in your environment

  • Prompts, tool inputs and tool outputs
  • Retrieved documents and memory contents
  • Secrets and credentials in the Context Vault
  • The decision ledger and its hash chain

Sent to Zenstra cloud

  • Agent names, owners and policy versions
  • Decision counts and latency, without payloads
  • Health of each decision point
  • Console sign-in events

Never collected

  • Member or patient records
  • Model weights or training data
  • Any data for training models, ours or a provider's
  • Anything you have not enabled for export
Security practices

How we protect the platform.

A summary of the controls behind the certifications. The SOC 2 report describes each one and how the auditor tested it.

{{icon:lock}}Data security

  • Encryption at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.2 or later
  • Each customer tenant isolated, with its own keys
  • Data residency in the US or EU

{{icon:code}}Secure development

  • Every change reviewed by a second engineer before release
  • Dependency and container scanning on every pull request
  • Signed releases for the SDK, gateway and endpoint app

{{icon:key}}Access control

  • Phishing-resistant MFA for every employee and contractor
  • Production access is just-in-time, approved and recorded
  • Access reviewed quarterly and removed the day someone leaves

{{icon:alert}}Testing and response

  • Annual third-party penetration test, with every finding retested
  • Incident response plan exercised twice a year
  • Customers notified of a security incident affecting their data without undue delay
