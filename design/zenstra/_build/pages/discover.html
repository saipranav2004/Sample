Three questions you should answer before the examiner asks.

You cannot govern an agent you do not know exists. Discovery is where every Zenstra deployment starts.

Q1

Which agents are acting in our environment?

Built, bought and shadow: custom agents, copilots, core-platform agents, MCP connectors and no-code flows.

Q2

Who owns each one?

Every agent gets a named owner, a declared purpose and an approval record before it reaches production.

Q3

What can each one reach?

Tool grants, credentials and data classes per agent, so the blast radius is known before something goes wrong.

Agent inventory and identity

One record, one owner and one key per agent.

Each agent gets a cryptographic identity, continuous attestation and instant revocation if its access drifts from what was approved. The record carries the owner, the declared purpose, the framework, the data classes it touches and who approved it.

  • Per-agent keys with automatic rotation
  • Attestation on every call, from known hosts only
  • Owners and approvers synchronised from your identity provider
