Privacy notice

Last updated 1 October 2026

In short

We collect what you give us on this website and what our product needs to run. Agent prompts and payloads stay in your environment. We never sell personal data or use customer data to train models.

What we collect

  • Contact details you submit in a form, such as your name, work email and company.
  • Account details for people who sign in to the Zenstra console.
  • Product metadata described in our Trust Center, such as agent names and decision counts.
  • Website usage data, only if you accept analytics cookies.

How we use it

To reply to your request, provide and secure the product, meet legal obligations and, with your consent, send you research updates. You can unsubscribe at any time.

Sharing

We share data only with the subprocessors listed in our Trust Center, under written agreements, and when the law requires it.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, or object to how we use it, by writing to privacy@zenstra.ai. We reply within 30 days.

Accessibility statement

Last updated 1 October 2026

In short

We aim for WCAG 2.2 AA on this website and in the Zenstra console, and we want to hear when we miss.

  • Every page works with a keyboard, with a visible focus indicator.
  • Animation stops when your device asks for reduced motion.
  • Light and dark themes both meet contrast requirements for text.
  • Status is never shown by colour alone.

Report a barrier to accessibility@zenstra.ai. We reply within five business days.
