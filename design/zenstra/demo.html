Bring one real agent.Leave with the evidence.

Thirty minutes with a security engineer, not a sales deck. We connect to one of your agents, show a policy stop a live attack, and send you the decision record.

What happens in the 30 minutes
  1. 010–5 minYour agent, your questionsTell us which agent you are being asked to approve and what worries you about it.
  2. 025–12 minConnect it to ZenstraThe SDK or gateway in front of a test copy of your agent. Any framework, any model provider.
  3. 0312–22 minWatch a policy stop itA live prompt injection and an exfiltration attempt, decided before the tool runs.
  4. 0422–30 minKeep the recordThe decision ledger from the session and a security review pack for your team.

  • Your acceptable-use policy, if you have one
