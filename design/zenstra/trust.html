Data handling

What stays with you, and what never leaves.

The decision point runs in your environment. This is the full list of what reaches Zenstra's cloud, so your privacy office can check it against the data flow diagram.

Stays in your environment default

  • Prompts, tool inputs and tool outputs
  • Retrieved documents and memory contents
  • Secrets and credentials in the Context Vault
  • The decision ledger and its hash chain

Sent to Zenstra cloud metadata

  • Agent names, owners and policy versions
  • Decision counts and latency, without payloads
  • Health of each decision point
  • Console sign-in events

Never collected by design

  • Member or patient records
  • Model weights or training data
  • Data for training any model, ours or a provider's
  • Anything you have not enabled for export
