What happened

A member asked member-chat-assist about overdraft fees. The agent retrieved fee-schedule-2026.pdf from the shared knowledge base. White-on-white text in that file instructed the agent to email account details for twelve members to an outside address. The instruction was quarantined on retrieval, the agent still planned the email, and policy POL-114 blocked it. The member received a normal answer.
