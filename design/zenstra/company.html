About us

Security teams were being asked to approve agents they could not see.

Agents now read member records, call internal tools and take actions on their own. The controls most institutions have were built for people and for text: identity providers issue tokens, AI firewalls filter prompts. Neither decides whether a specific agent should take a specific action at the moment it tries.

Zenstra makes that decision, in the customer's own environment, on every action, and keeps a record anyone can verify. We started with credit unions because their examiners ask the hardest questions about third-party automation, and the answers they need are the answers every regulated institution will need.

01

Decide before the action, not after.

Alerts about something that already happened do not protect a member.

02

Run where the data lives.

Our product sits inside your environment, so your data does not have to come to us.

03

Evidence anyone can check.

A record you have to trust us about is not evidence. The ledger can be verified without us.

04

Say what is not done yet.
