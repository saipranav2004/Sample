Runtime detection

Each agent measured against its own baseline.

Agents change hour by hour. Zenstra learns each agent's normal tools, data reach and call volume, and flags drift mid-task, before a gradual widening of scope becomes an incident.

  • Drift scores per agent, with the run that triggered them
  • New tools and new data classes flagged the first time they appear
  • Detections ranked by severity and blast radius
Investigations

What happened, how bad it is, what to do next.

Each detection opens on its outcome and blast radius, then the agent run step by step, then ranked response actions that say whether they can be undone.

Open the investigation in the console
Compliance evidence

Exam-ready, because it was recorded as it happened.

Your inventory, least-privilege grants and decision trail export straight to your SIEM and into evidence packs mapped to the frameworks your auditors use.

  • Streaming export to Splunk, Microsoft Sentinel and webhooks
  • Tickets opened in ServiceNow from detections
  • Delivery buffered locally if your SIEM is unreachable
Evidence packs

Hand over the pack, not a spreadsheet.

Each pack is generated from the ledger for a date range and signed, so the reader can check it was not edited afterwards.
