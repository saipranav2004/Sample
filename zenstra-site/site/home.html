One inline layer between your agents and everything they touch.

Calls come in from users, AI agents and peer agents. They leave for MCP servers, external tools, APIs and enterprise systems. Zenstra sits in the line between, with no agent rewrite.

Inside: four checks, in series.

Every call passes all four, in order, at 18 ms p95. A call that fails any one of them goes no further, while legitimate calls keep flowing.
