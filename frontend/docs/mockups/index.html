Screen 1 - Sign in

Full-bleed flat navy; the credential card floats on it at the right, and both columns start on the same line. Every colour here was sampled and every size measured off the supplied design - canvas #0a192f, action #1492c4, accent #53c9ed, body #94a3b8, field #f8fafc - and in the app each one is a clamp() calibrated to hit the measured value at the design's own width. "Forgot password?", "Onboard Tenant" and "Try it now" are absent: no endpoint backs any of them.

Screen 3 - Identity explorer

Facet rail with live counts from the scan summary, preset view tabs, and row actions that appear on hover. The rail closes and the records take the freed width; the choice persists per viewer. Record-surface tools are an icon button for refresh, a settings popover for density, and an overflow menu for export - Carbon and PatternFly both cap a table toolbar at two visible buttons and move the rest to an overflow menu, and Cloudscape puts display preferences behind collection preferences.
